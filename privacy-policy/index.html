1. Interpretation & Definitions

Interpretation

Words with initial capital letters have meanings defined below. These definitions apply whether they appear in singular or plural form.

Definitions

  • App – The HaiTv mobile application.
  • Developer / We / Us / Our – The individual or entity that operates HaiTv.
  • You / User – Any individual who downloads or uses the App.
  • Personal Data – Any information that can identify a natural person directly or indirectly.
  • Usage Data – Anonymized, aggregated data collected automatically when you use the App (e.g., crash logs, feature interactions).
  • Device – Any smartphone, tablet, or electronic device used to access the App.
  • IPTV – Internet Protocol Television; streaming content delivered via internet protocols.
  • Playlist / Stream URL – User-supplied URLs pointing to IPTV content sources.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

HaiTv does not require user registration or personal accounts. Any IPTV playlist URLs, stream URLs, or credentials you enter in the App are stored locally on your device only. We never transmit, store, or access this information on our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Certain technical data may be collected automatically to maintain and improve the App:

  • Device Information – Device model, OS version, and non-personally identifiable device identifiers.
  • Usage Data – Anonymized feature interaction data, crash reports, and performance metrics. This data cannot identify you personally.
  • Network Information – Basic network status (Wi-Fi / mobile data) used solely to optimize streaming performance.

2.3 App Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions to function correctly:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Fetches IPTV streams and related data over the internet — fundamental to the App's core function.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether an internet connection is available, used to optimize data usage.
CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Enables Wi-Fi Multicast mode for local network device discovery and certain streaming protocols.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Runs a foreground service to keep media playback active when you navigate away from the App.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Posts playback controls and relevant app notifications to the notification panel.
DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION A custom internal permission for secure component communication within the App.
DUMP Associated with androidx.profileinstaller for app profiling and performance optimization.

3. How We Use Your Data

The limited data collected by HaiTv is used exclusively for:

  • App Functionality – Enabling core IPTV playback, playlist management, and streaming features.
  • Performance Improvement – Analyzing anonymized crash reports and usage patterns to fix bugs and enhance the user experience.
  • Security & Stability – Protecting the App from threats and ensuring stable operation.
We never use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating and improving the App.

4. Retention Periods

All data stored locally on your device (playlist URLs, watch history, favourites) remains on your device until you uninstall the App or manually clear its data.

Anonymized usage and crash data retained by third-party analytics tools is kept only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, after which it is deleted or anonymized further.

We do not retain personally identifiable data on our servers because we do not collect it in the first place.

5. Data Transfers

HaiTv is developed and operated from West Bengal, India. If you access the App from outside India, please be aware that limited anonymized technical data may be processed in India or by servers operated by third-party analytics providers, which may be located in other jurisdictions.

By using the App, you consent to the processing of anonymized data as described in this policy, regardless of where you are located. We ensure that any such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

6. Your Rights & Deletion

Since HaiTv does not collect personally identifiable information, most traditional data-subject rights (access, rectification, portability) apply only to the local data stored on your own device, which you control directly.

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Delete Local Data – Clear the App's storage via your device's Settings → Apps → HaiTv → Clear Data.
  • Delete All Data – Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored information.
  • Opt Out of Analytics – Contact us at the email below to request exclusion from anonymized analytics collection.
  • Raise a Grievance – Contact the Grievance Officer listed in Section 12 for any privacy concern.

We will respond to all verifiable requests within 30 days as required under applicable Indian law (IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023).

7. Disclosure of Data

HaiTv does not sell, rent, or trade your information with third parties. Because we do not collect personal IPTV credentials or viewing data, there is no such information to disclose.

We may share anonymized, aggregated usage data with third-party analytics providers solely to understand usage patterns and improve the App. This data cannot identify you personally.

Data may be disclosed when required by law if we are in good faith belief that such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation or valid court/government order.
  • Protect and defend the rights or property of HaiTv.
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the App.
  • Ensure the personal safety of users or the public.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information stored locally on your device and any anonymized data we process. These measures include secure coding practices, use of HTTPS for any data in transit, and regular security reviews.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. We encourage you to use a secure device lock and keep your OS updated.

9. Children's Privacy

HaiTv is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 18 where a higher age threshold applies under local law). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us immediately at the email address below. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child, we will take prompt steps to delete it from our records.

10. Third-Party Links & Services

HaiTv is a player and does not provide IPTV content. The content you access comes from third-party IPTV providers or sources that you configure. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party services.

The App may integrate with third-party services such as Google Play Services for functionalities like Chromecast / Cast support. These services operate under their own privacy policies (e.g., Google Privacy Policy).

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party IPTV provider or service you choose to use with HaiTv.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's features, legal requirements, or best practices. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date at the top of this page.

For material changes, we will provide a prominent notice within the App or via notification. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
